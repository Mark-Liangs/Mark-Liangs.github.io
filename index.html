Biography

Currently I am a first year Ph.D. student of School of Intelligence Science and Technology in Nanjing University and a member of LAMDA Group, led by professor Zhi-Hua Zhou.

I received my B.Sc. degree in Statistics from School of Mathematics, Sichuan University in June 2024. In the same year, I was admitted to study for a M.Sc. degree in Nanjing University under the supervision of Assistant Professor Shao-Qun Zhang.

Research Interests

My research interests mainly include topics in Machine Learning, especially Uncertainty Analysis, involving uncertainty quantification and optimization in
  • Deep Neural Networks
  • Large Language Models (LLMs), particularly uncertainty in reasoning
  • LLM-driven Agents
